How it works & tracking limitations

Choose a video your browser supports (MP4 / H.264 recommended). Frames are sampled at regular intervals, independently of playback speed. Extend your fingers to draw and curl them to lift the brush, just like the original Finger Ribbons. Small hands, occlusion, fast movements, and cuts may cause missed detections. Up to two hands are tracked. The model may take a moment to load. Pause at any time, or adjust settings and draw again. Drawing again replaces the current artwork, so export it first. Unsaved artwork is lost when you close this page.
